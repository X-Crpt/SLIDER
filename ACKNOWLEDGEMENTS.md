# Acknowledgements

## CVA6

SLIDER is built on top of [CVA6](https://github.com/openhwgroup/cva6),
developed by the OpenHW Group and originally by ETH Zurich / University
of Bologna (PULP Platform). `cva6/ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md` and
`cva6/CODEOWNERS` list CVA6's own upstream contributors and are
preserved unmodified.

## This work

The SLH-DSA/SPHINCS+ coprocessor (`rtl/spx-unit/`), the Keccak
coprocessor (`rtl/keccak_ip/`), and the associated software tests
(`sw/`) were developed by Behnam Farnaghinejad, Valeria Piscopo,
Alessandra Dolmeta, Ernesto Sanchez, Stefano Di Carlo, Alessandro
Savino, Maurizio Martina, and Guido Masera at Politecnico di Torino,
Departments of Electronics and Telecommunications, and of Control and
Computer Engineering.

`rtl/spx-unit/hw/keccak/` reuses the HORCRUX Keccak-f[1600] permutation
core verbatim; see `rtl/spx-unit/README.md` for details.
